Context

Pepagora is a B2B marketplace that connects buyers and suppliers. Version 1.0 met the core listing and enquiry needs, and the company wanted version 2.0 to use AI to make discovery and matching smarter and to compete with larger B2B platforms.

What I did

  • Studied competing B2B marketplaces and their AI features to find gaps and opportunities for Pepagora.
  • Gathered requirements from leadership and internal teams and turned them into BRDs, PRDs, epics and user stories with clear acceptance criteria.

The team got a documented, prioritized AI-led direction for Pepagora 2.0, with requirements engineering could build from sprint by sprint.

Healthcare analytics · 2023 – 2025

Medical Billing & Claims Enhancement

Inrika Inc · US healthcare client

Role
Business Analyst / Scrum Master
Team
Client stakeholders, developers, QA
Method
Agile / Scrum
TableauPower BIGoogle AnalyticsSQLWorkflow docs

Context

The client's billing team had little visibility into how claims moved through approval and payment. Claim denials, billing errors and slow payment cycles were hard to measure, which made it hard to know where to improve.

What I did

  • Mapped the end-to-end claims process from submission through review and approval to payment, and documented each step and handoff.
  • Worked with stakeholders to define the KPIs that mattered: claim approval rate, billing accuracy and payment cycle time.
  • Built Tableau, Power BI and Google Analytics dashboards so the team could track those KPIs and spot problem areas.
  • Wrote user stories and acceptance criteria for the enhancements and coordinated testing and UAT with QA.

Outcome

The client got one clear view of claims performance and a documented workflow to base process improvements on.

Context

The bank's risk team needed a faster way to spot suspicious activity. Anomalies were spread across transaction data, which made it slow to see which accounts and regions needed attention.

What I did

  • Gathered requirements from the risk team on the signals they watch and the decisions they make with them.
  • Designed user flows and UI prototypes in Figma and Adobe XD to agree the screens with stakeholders before development started.

  • Turned the agreed designs into user stories for developers and supported QA through testing and UAT.

The risk team got a prototype everyone had signed off on, and dashboards that show high-risk activity in one place.

Banking · Compliance · 2023 – 2025

AML Compliance Enhancement

Inrika Inc · Banking client

Role
Business Analyst / Scrum Master
Team
Compliance, product owners, developers
Method
Agile / Scrum
Process flowsBusiness rulesSOPsAPI requirementsVisio / Lucidchart

Context

The bank needed to strengthen its anti-money-laundering (AML) controls: monitoring transactions, raising Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) and connecting the monitoring system to core banking.

What I did

  • Analyzed the existing compliance workflow with the compliance team to find gaps and manual steps.
  • Created process flows covering how a transaction is monitored, flagged, investigated and reported.
  • Documented the business rules for transaction monitoring and the requirements for SAR filing.
  • Wrote requirements for the API integration with the core banking system so the right data reached the monitoring tool.
  • Prepared SOPs so compliance staff could follow the new process consistently.

Outcome

The client got clear, documented AML rules, flows and SOPs that both the compliance team and the developers could work from.
